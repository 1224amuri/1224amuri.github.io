I am drawn to the study of living systems and the remarkable adaptations that life evolves to persist and flourish. My work explores how humans and our technologies might coexist more thoughtfully with the rest of the natural world. Through research, fieldwork, workshops, and writing, I try to design spaces where people can reflect on their place within the wider web of life. I am motivated by questions about the futures we are building—and who or what those futures make space for.
